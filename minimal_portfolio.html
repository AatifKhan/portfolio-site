I’m Aatif. I design and build internet things — often for the teams and companies I work with, sometimes for myself.

Currently leading design at Zensar. My path has taken me through roles at LTI, GeoSpoc (an OLA company), and iAuro, focusing on the intersection of craft and utility.

I’m based in Pune with my wife and our son. Lately, I’ve been figuring out the balance between shipping projects and raising a five-year-old, learning just as much from the tiny human as I do from design.

Craft over noise.
