  1. Toolbar-led

    The tools were visible, but there was a lot to take in at once.

  2. Sidebar navigation

    The sidebar gave tools a predictable home. The labels still described tools rather than people’s questions.

  3. Configuration dialogs

    There was room for settings, but the dialog covered the place being studied.

  4. Guided steps

    Guided steps helped with setup, but made it harder to explore freely or compare places.

  5. Scorecard-first

    A score gave a quick summary, but the map became secondary. Keep the score close to the location.

  6. Question-led workspace

    Organise navigation around people’s questions. Keep details beside the map and the location in view.
