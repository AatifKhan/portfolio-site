# Reference mapping before refinement

The source is the approved 1448 × 1086 Corner Café image. A fresh 1500 × 1286 browser capture of the current scene is saved as `before.jpg` (1448 × 1086 art crop at x26/y96). Four equal-scale source/before comparison inputs were opened and inspected: park, café, office, and east neighborhood. The source is always on the left.

1. **Neighborhood overview — needs refinement.** Five building types, five cars and the basic street arrangement are present, but the café façade, office column count, house rows and apartment rows drift from the source. This is an illustration-fidelity audit; typography and core controls are unchanged by the target image.
2. **Park close-up — poor match.** The reference has three benches, including one perpendicular to the other two. The implementation has four identical-orientation benches. Backrests are too tall, seat depth/legs differ, the front round tree is too high, and the park conifer is too short and too far forward. Bushes are sparse balls on undersized rims rather than broad, scalloped flowering masses. Several stems are too long. Evidence: `park-before-comparison.png`.
3. **Café close-up — needs refinement.** The source has two wide upper windows on each visible face; the implementation has three narrow windows on the left. Source seating has three chairs per table and five visible guests across three tables. The current tables are small, with two chairs each and two guests total. Flowering planters, café-side conifers, cap-height street lamps and bollards need measured placement. Source coffee equipment has a broad front face with two group heads, a cup rail and a fluted counter. Evidence: `cafe-before-comparison.png`.
4. **Office and east buildings — needs refinement.** The office's left façade has three columns across six rows, versus four. The source has quiet window silhouettes, not repeated prominent plants. The house has two rows of larger windows and an entry replacing a lower window; the apartment has three rows, a lower middle entry and two closed balcony fronts with fine rails. The current house/apartment have extra rows. The roof tank is missing its slatted platform and domed lid. Evidence: `office-before-comparison.png`, `east-before-comparison.png`.
5. **Streets and small objects — needs refinement.** Source storm drains are recessed filled grates rather than empty rectangles; poles need white centers, capped elliptical heads and base rings; the menu board has a broad inset face; the curb needs a single clear raised edge. Source cars are boxier hatchbacks with larger wheels, lights, grille and a short hood. These are measured separately from foliage to avoid hiding errors in an overall scene judgment.

Accessible controls, focus and screen-size behavior must be retained. Screenshot inspection alone does not establish accessibility compliance or physical touch behavior. No raster illustration will be inserted into the live scene to disguise geometry differences. The generated source contains small perspective inconsistencies; hidden sides and subpixel marks require interpretation. Measured anchors in `scripts/geomarketeer-neighborhood-details.js` distinguish visible image coordinates from world dimensions.
